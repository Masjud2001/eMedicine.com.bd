# eMedicine - User Guide

A brief guide on the primary user flows for the eMedicine platform.

---

## 💊 Ordering Medicine

### **For OTC (Non-Prescription) Drugs**
1. **Search**: Use the main search bar to find your medicine.
2. **Shop**: Browse the `/shop` page using filters for categories and brands.
3. **Cart**: Add desired items to your healthcare basket. Click the cart icon to view the bag.
4. **Checkout**: Provide your shipping details and choose a payment method (bKash, Nagad, or COD).
5. **Confirm**: Place the order and receive your identifier (e.g., #MED-12345).

---

## 📄 Prescription Upload

### **How to Upload**
1. **Navigate**: Go to the `/upload-prescription` page or use the CTA on the homepage.
2. **Submit**: Drag and drop your image (JPEG, PNG) or select a file.
3. **Verify**: Provide your delivery phone number and any specific notes for the pharmacist.
4. **Review**: Our licensed pharmacist will review the order and contact you for confirmation.

---

## 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Consultation

### **Booking a Virtual Visit**
1. **Directory**: Go to the `/consultation` page to see available specialists.
2. **Selection**: Choose a doctor based on specialty, rating, experience, or price.
3. **Book**: Click "Book Consult Now" or use the calendar icon to schedule an appointment.
4. **Connect**: Use the chat or video features within the platform (tele-medicine verified).

---

## 🚚 Tracking Your Order

### **Live Logistics Tracking**
1. **Unique ID**: Locate your Order ID from your SMS or email.
2. **Track**: Go to the `/track-order` page and enter the identifier.
3. **Status**: View real-time progress steps: Order Received → Verified → Picked Up → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
4. **Rider Info**: See the assigned rider's name, photo, and click the phone icon to call for updates.

---

## ♻️ Reordering (Auto-Refill)

### **Quick Medication Refills**
1. **Search**: Enter your previous Order ID or Phone Number into the Refill Request tool.
2. **Reorder**: Select the previous items you wish to refill.
3. **Express**: Use the streamlined refill process for faster checkout.
